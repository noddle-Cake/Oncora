## Identity after standardization (refines D-01)

- 3,945 standardized parents span more than one *source* connectivity block, mostly salt forms registered separately (e.g. a drug and its mesylate). Their features are identical, so splitting by the source block would leak. **Splits group by the connectivity block of the standardized parent (`std_inchi_key[:14]`).**
- 65,412 (standardized parent, target) groups have more than one row in `target_pairs`, 8,707 with conflicting labels: stereoisomers or salt forms measured separately, with identical achiral features. Spec 05 collapses them before training (measured beats sampled; label from the median pChEMBL).

Rows are keyed by the source InChIKey used in the modeling tables. Where standardization changes the key (salts stripped, charges neutralized, stereo absent from the source SMILES), the features describe the standardized parent, which is what the model sees at inference.
